About BestPortableCharger.com

Independent reviews from people who actually test what they recommend.

Who We Are

We're a small team of tech enthusiasts who got tired of "review" sites that just rewrite Amazon listings. Every portable charger we recommend has been purchased with our own money, tested in our lab, and used in real life before it makes our list.

Our Testing Process

We don't just read spec sheets. Every power bank goes through:

  • Capacity verification — We discharge each unit through a calibrated USB power meter to measure actual deliverable capacity.
  • Speed testing — Charging speed measured at multiple battery levels on standardized test devices.
  • Thermal testing — Surface temperature monitoring during maximum output.
  • Real-world carry — At least one week of daily use to assess ergonomics and reliability.
  • Longevity checks — We revisit products after 6 months to check for degradation.

Our Editorial Standards

We don't accept free products from manufacturers. We don't run sponsored content. Our recommendations are based solely on testing performance and value. When we link to products on Amazon, we earn a small commission — but this never influences our rankings or recommendations.
